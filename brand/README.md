# MYRQVIA — Identity 01

**The quiet orbit.**

A single open orbit with one satellite point. The gap is the distance; the dot is the message crossing it. The mark is deliberately not a letter, a paper plane, a speech bubble, or a borrowed messaging icon.

## Construction

- 64 × 64 mark artboard.
- 21-unit orbit radius, 6.5-unit stroke.
- The orbit is open for 80° at the point where the message leaves.
- One 4.2-unit dot sits in the opening, outside the orbit.
- The complete mark is rotated −12° for a less mechanical, more natural rhythm.
- Custom monoline wordmark: geometric N/O/V/A paths, 6-unit stroke, rounded terminals.

## Clear space & minimum size

- Clear space: at least the diameter of the dot on all sides.
- App icon: 1024 master; export at 512, 256, 128.
- Mark minimum: 24 px digital / 8 mm print.
- Primary lockup minimum: 120 px digital / 30 mm print.

## Originality / legal note

The mark was drawn from original geometry for this project. It intentionally contains:

- no Telegram paper-plane silhouette
- no plain “N” monogram
- no speech-bubble clone
- no Google Gemini-style four-point sparkle
- no third-party icon trace

This is a design-originality statement, **not a legal clearance**. Before commercial launch, run a trademark search for the **MYRQVIA** name and the logo in your target classes and jurisdictions. Trademark availability cannot be guaranteed by a design file alone.
